## Design Context

### Users

Fieldwork is used by a lifter during a workout: standing, holding a phone in one hand,
between sets, with divided attention and possibly chalky or sweaty hands. The primary job is
to move through a prescribed session, record a set in one or two taps, and preserve an accurate
history without judging performance.

### Brand Personality

Calm, durable, direct. The product should feel like a dependable training instrument rather
than a motivational coach, social fitness product, or gamified habit tracker.

### Aesthetic Direction

Maintain the established deep-slate instrument-panel direction, exact light/dark palette, and
mobile-first portrait layout. Actual logging controls lead the hierarchy; prescription remains
legible reference material. Avoid visual redesign, decorative effects, and color-only state.

### Design Principles

- Keep the current set and its primary action reachable without scrolling or precise aiming.
- Make every state change visibly perceivable while retaining screen-reader announcements.
- Preserve authored structure while allowing the user to adapt exercise order in the gym.
- Distinguish prescribed, unsaved, saved, skipped, and failed states without relying on color.
- Prefer direct, honest outcome copy over motivational, evaluative, or storage-centric language.
